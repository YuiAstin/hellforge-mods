# Notices

Hina is built on the kit base (`src/kitbase/`, shared by the author's character kits). Its hit-result layout, the
hooked game functions and their instruction guards, the entity descriptor lookup and the scene-graph position read are
taken from **Helldiver Survival Rework 0.3.2** by enochlophobianumerophobia (https://hf-mod.com/Mods/Details/14), MIT
licensed: its licence is kept in LICENSE-Helldiver-Survival-Rework.txt. The head scene-graph node (90) comes from Scope
of Authority's Apex knockdown notes. Their research in turn used **HD2Runtime by SkyeShade**
(https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime).

Her view is the camera's pose as **Apache - Rise Above** by RSNLRiggs (after Khi) reads it (the unit object's world,
+0x230920), used with permission. The settings rows use **Mod Options Menu** by CowboyBingus (0BSD) through its public
API. The HUD uses HD2Runtime's public overlay API, and its skill panel follows the stratagem list through
**HD2Runtime**'s slot overlay (internal modules).

Her shield is the kit base's shield pool (`kb_allies.h`, from Alva). Her gun is put in her hands the way W's King of
Hearts does it (the Wield Probe's research), and Ishbosheth's fan follows Koleda's Full Speed cone and tracers. The
game-code research behind her reloads and her aim (the weapon managers, the game's set-loaded-rounds, the aim state)
is the author's own, from the game's code; HD2Runtime's field notes named the aim state first.

Hina (Sorasaki Hina) is a character of **Blue Archive** (NEXON Games / Yostar). This is an unofficial fan mod; no
character model is included. The three skill icons (hud-src/images) and the mod icon are Blue Archive art, © NEXON
Games / Yostar.
