# Notices

Executor is built on the kit base (`src/kitbase/`, shared by the author's character kits). Its hit-result layout, the
hooked game functions and their instruction guards, the entity descriptor lookup and the scene-graph position read are
taken from **Helldiver Survival Rework 0.3.2** by enochlophobianumerophobia (https://hf-mod.com/Mods/Details/14), MIT
licensed: its licence is kept in LICENSE-Helldiver-Survival-Rework.txt. Their research in turn used **HD2Runtime by
SkyeShade** (https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime).

His health record read and write (the health manager and its records) is HD2Runtime's research, as the author's Koleda
kit uses it. The heal, limb mending, projectiles and explosions are HellForge's own actions. Where his free rounds fly
is the camera's pose as **Apache - Rise Above** by RSNLRiggs (after Khi) reads it, used with permission, with the game's
raycast as Helldiver Survival Rework's cover check calls it. The settings rows use **Mod Options Menu** by CowboyBingus
(0BSD) through its public API. The HUD uses HD2Runtime's public overlay API, and its skill panel follows the stratagem
list through **HD2Runtime**'s slot overlay (internal modules).

Executor the Ex Foedere is a character of Arknights (Hypergryph / Yostar). This is an unofficial fan mod; no character
model or game art is included (the skill icons are drawn for this mod).
