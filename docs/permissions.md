Who has said we may reuse their code or assets, what they asked for in return, and where it came from. Check here before
shipping anything built on someone else's work, and put their credit in that mod's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.

## Notes

- Exosuit Concept Mech is marked Deprecated (1.2.1, 20 May 2026). On 3 Nov 2026 Killerco said he won't post the files
  publicly, but will give all of the mod's files and the mod page to someone who wants to maintain it. His source files
  would make re-exporting the mech for Koleda much easier than working from the shipped patch.
- Known issues users report on it: the damaged model is missing (the mech vanishes except the arms), a LUT issue that
  drops some texture detail, about 9 GB VRAM, legs that clip.

- Killercat no longer mods HD2 and doesn't check Nexus. If we fix or extend one of his mods, he offers edit rights on
  that mod page; contact him on Discord (`killerco`, or @ him in the HD2 modding Discord).
- Concept Mech as shipped replaces every vanilla mech. For Koleda it gets re-exported as a unit under our own name, so
  vanilla mechs keep their look.
