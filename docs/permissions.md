# Permissions to reuse other modders' work

Who has said we may reuse their code or assets, what they asked for in return, and where it came from. Check here before
shipping anything built on someone else's work, and put their credit in that mod's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.

| Author | Covers | Terms | Source | Recorded |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Killercat (Discord `killerco`) | All his Helldivers 2 mods, including Concept Mech (Nexus mod 7974), the model planned for Koleda's summon | Blanket permission to edit and reupload however we wish. Credit the original mod and credit him | His sticky post on his Nexus mods, 21 Oct 2026; Yui confirmed it applies to Concept Mech | 2026-11-04 |
| Beelzebub (giblett) | Beelzebub's Strat Pack code (exosuit weapon arms, own-record entities, custom units) | OK to use his code; credit him | Told to Yui directly | 2026-11-04 |
| Th3chef | Chef Shared Loader code (camera / world-to-screen) | OK to use; credit "Parts adapted from Chef Shared Loader by Th3chef" | Told to Yui directly | 2026-11-04 |

## Notes

- Killercat no longer mods HD2 and doesn't check Nexus. If we fix or extend one of his mods, he offers edit rights on
  that mod page; contact him on Discord (`killerco`, or @ him in the HD2 modding Discord).
- Concept Mech as shipped replaces every vanilla mech. For Koleda it gets re-exported as a unit under our own name, so
  vanilla mechs keep their look.
