# Leva (yui.leva) design notes
- Ult on cooldown (no Confectance: team resource in GFL2; Fox's Scheme zaps may shave cooldown).
- Blitz: one free use after each ult (same key, short window). Blitz = AOE in a straight line in front of her:
  spawn shell/coordinate points along a line, then arcs from those points.
- Ult: spawn shells/points around her (360), arcs from there, Voltage zones.
- Skill 1 Rational Suppression: rectangle AOE spreading across her view like Eagle Airstrike, electric
  (shells in a rectangle + arcs, or electric version of the strike). Negative Charge + paralysis.
- Skill 2 Orderly Disruption: AOE arc originating from the enemy she aims at (like Faelynn's Triple Maul AimTarget).
- Positive Charge on herself (small heal + more dmg on charged), Code stacks, Voltage zones, T-Doll package.
