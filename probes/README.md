# Probes

Research code with no gameplay purpose, kept for what it found. Build 25480438 only.

| Probe | What it found |
|---|---|
| `warp-probe/` | A HellForge mod that hooks the LIFT-182 Warp Pack's position setter (`game.dll+0x5A2BB0`) and launch flag (`+0x5A3D80`) with pass-through detours and logs every call. It showed the warp calls the setter from `+0x88D795` with (NULL, the Helldiver, the destination) and then zeroes velocity, which is how Faelynn's Loyal Dash and Lenna's Leaping Pursuit teleport. Drop the folder into `HellForge/mods` to run it. |
| `groundat.c` | The ground finder (a downward ray through the game's own raycast, skipping the caster and slivers) that keeps dashes and leaps out of walls and from landing underground. A snippet from the mods, not a standalone file. |
| `keytest.c` | A desktop test of the .ini key parser (`Shift+Q`, `Ctrl+Alt+F`, `F5`, `Mouse4`, `#81`). Build with any C compiler and run. |
| `data/faelynn-hit-probe.log` | A Faelynn probe build's log of raw hit records before and after the hit calculation; it located the armour-penetration floats at `+0x9C/+0xA0/+0xA4` that Corrosion raises. |
| `frv-package-probe/` | The call-in package at stratagem row +0xA8 of the three FRVs, read from copies of their stratagems on HellForge 0.5.0-beta (2026-11-25): row 26 M-103 Supply `0x149F685717737CAE`, row 105 M-102 Gunner `0x0BDF199F7AC14F43`, row 135 M-104 Incinerator `0x2CEE40DFBD0B174D`; row +0xF8 is 0 on all three. |
| `wield-probe/` | For W's King of Hearts: a spawned GL-21 put in the Helldiver's hands with 40 rounds, and her own weapon given back (played 2026-11-25 on HellForge 0.5.0-beta, 0.8.0's try 4). What works: `RequestEntitySpawn` of the weapon's entity with its LoadoutPackage loaded, 0.75 s for its spawn to finish, the inventory's own take-into-slot (`game.dll+0x9AE030`: inventory, Helldiver, item, slot 3 = support, 0; it carries the item on her back through `0x9AA290` and `0x8BF870`), then the Helldiver's own switch request (`0xA93430`, on the object a switch by hand passes it: her entity at +0, the wanted selection at +4), which stows her weapon (`AttachToUnit` node 27 / 11 / 30 from `0x8C06A3`) and draws the new one to her hand (node 84 from `0x8C0B70`). `AddMagazineRounds` past the capacity holds (40 in a 10-round GL-21, fired down to 11). Back: the request for her earlier selection, `0x9A8840` to let the item go (it leaves the slot's entry: cleared by hand), `RemoveEntity`, `WeaponRefill`. What does not: `Wield` or `SwitchWeapon` called directly (the Helldiver keeps its own wanted selection: no draw, and it switches back within half a second); the held-item slot (`0x9AB950`: a weapon there crashes the Helldiver's update at `0xA99016`); a spawned weapon cannot be picked up by hand until it has been dropped once. 0.9.0 ran it as the kit would, three cases played (no support weapon; one on her back; one in her hands): her switch-request object is the Avatar manager's `+0x53DCD0 + 0x1238 x` her index (the same address a switch by hand passes), and a support weapon she carries is dropped by the take, removed, and a new one of its resource spawned and taken when the skill ends. |

Leva's lockdown damage probe (every hit and kill near her first lockdown of a mission) is still in `mods/leva/src/leva.c`; its findings on arc hits are in [docs/research/game-notes.md](../docs/research/game-notes.md).
